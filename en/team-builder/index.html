How to Use the Team Builder

  1. Choose Player 1 or Player 2, then select a Tatari family and one of its available evolution tiers. In Zombie Rush mode, you can toggle level display and select up to three chip types for each player.
  2. Tap a board cell to place it. On desktop, you can also drag a list image or tier onto an empty cell. Each player can normally place 10 Tatari, or 11 when Placement limit +1 is on.
  3. The board uses T1 images, with T1–T4 at bottom right and P1/P2 at top left. Levels appear at bottom left only when level display is enabled in Zombie Rush mode.
  4. Tap an occupied cell to edit it or copy it to the other player. Drag on the board to move or swap it, or use the minus button at the top right to remove it immediately.
  5. Save multiple formations on this device or share them through a short URL, image or text. Existing share links and legacy saves remain compatible.

Share URLs contain players, tiers, positions and unlock settings, plus the level-display preference and selected chips in Zombie Rush mode. Formation names and contents are not sent to our server or Analytics.
