How to Use the Team Builder

  1. Choose a Tatari family and an available evolution tier. Only Zombie Rush uses Player 1 and Player 2 and supports levels, up to three chip types per Player, and cap unlocks.
  2. On mobile, choosing a tier slides the picker away to the right. Tap a board cell or drag the selected Tatari image onto an empty cell. To choose again, tap Select Tatari at the top center or inside the board. You can also drag from the handle beside each tier.
  3. The placement limit is 15 Tatari for Free, Normal, and Boss Rally, and 5 for Badge Dojo. Only Zombie Rush uses a per-Player limit of 10, or 11 when Placement limit +1 has been acquired and enabled.
  4. The board shows the T1 image and the selected T1–T4 tier at bottom right. P1/P2 and levels appear only in Zombie Rush.
  5. Tap a filled cell to change its tier, move it, or remove it. In Zombie Rush, you can also change the Player or copy it to the other Player.
  6. Save multiple formations on this device or share them through a short URL, image or text. Existing share links and legacy saves remain compatible.

Share URLs include evolution tiers and positions. In Zombie Rush they also include Players, cap unlocks, level display settings, and selected chips. Formation names and content are not sent to our server or Analytics.
