    How to Use the Team Builder

    1. Choose an empty slot, select an evolution stage, and place a Tatari.
    2. Choose a mode such as Normal, Zombie Rush or Badge Dojo to review its Tier distribution and role breakdown.
    3. Save the team on this device, or export it as a share link, image or text.
    4. If you want feedback, review and edit the team before opening the Q&A Board. Nothing is posted automatically.

    Share links contain only family IDs, evolution stages, placement and mode. Team data is not sent to this site's server or Analytics.
