How to Read the Tier List

SSSStrong in multiple contents and has high long-term development value. Even if you grow it as a priority, it will not rot easily.
SSVery strong. In certain modes, it may work even better than SSS.
SStrong enough. Top class depending on role, attributes, and content.
Evaluation pendingIt does not mean that it is weak. At present, there is insufficient evidence to give an overall ranking.

Example of evaluation changing depending on mode

ネコオリ

Although it is a general SS, it is the highest class for beginners, regular, and dojo. The third evolution is fast, and the training efficiency in the early stages is very high.

ビリジカ

SSS in Zombie Rush. Penetration, paralysis, and binding are useful in multiple modes, so this re-examination has updated them to comprehensive SSS.

ポタカゲ

Comprehensive SS. Especially in the Badge Dojo, survival skills that utilize stealth are powerful.
