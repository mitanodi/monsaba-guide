How to Read the Tier List

SSSStrong in multiple contents and has high long-term development value. Even if you grow it as a priority, it will not rot easily.
SSVery strong. In certain modes, it may work even better than SSS.
SStrong enough. Top class depending on role, attributes, and content.
Evaluation pendingIt does not mean that it is weak. At present, there is insufficient evidence to give an overall ranking.

Criteria:We separately review Normal, Zombie Rush, Dojo, beginner suitability, and roles after evolution using verified data.

Data sources:We cross-check in-game verified data with dated external sources.

The overall tier is a provisional evaluation based on the confirmed skill data on this site, compared with multiple domestic and international strategy information with confirmed update dates and mode differences. We do not directly transfer external tiers and do not use overseas evaluations where name correspondence cannot be confirmed as a basis for direct changes.
