Usage rules

In order to maintain a place where people can feel free to ask questions, the following posts are prohibited. Violating posts will be reviewed and deleted by the administrator.

  • Posts, vandalism, advertising/spam unrelated to Clash of Critters
  • Slander, discriminatory expressions, sexual and violent inappropriate posts
  • Personal information such as phone number, address, email address, real name, etc.
  • Impersonation of others, fraud and cheating related
  • Malicious guidance to external sites

Questions, answers, and any name will be made public. Please do not write personal information. Please report any problematic posts using the "Report" button on each post.

How This Differs from the Guide Assistant

Guide Assistant βis a function that automatically answers from data within the site. This bulletin board is a place for users to ask and answer questions freely.
