Evolution Priority & Recommended T3 Upgrades

In the early stages, it is efficient to prioritize Tatari, which has fewer stars required for third evolution. However, priorities will change depending on the long-term development value and content.

Towards evolutionary diagnosis

Managed and verified byおぢ

Evolution is judged by dividing "strength of Tatari" and "value of using material at that stage"

The shortest T3 is ネコオリ / ヒモリ / フルッグ. The runner-up is ガオデン / ガントル (Initial: コロカメ) / ヒマワリン. For long-term cultivation, フレイムシ (initial: ヒノムシ) / ヒマワリン / ヤンザル / プラビ are candidates.

Overall Tatari Tier Zombie Rush Strategy Recommendations by attribute

Who did the third evolution come from? T3 cost performance priority

This is a training guide based on in-game data and public strategy information. This is not an official ranking.

SSS: Top priority

Since it is possible to aim for the third evolution with significantly fewer stars than the normal 18-star group, the efficiency of strengthening the force in the early stages is high.

ネコオリ familyT3: チルレオ / 6 starsヒモリ familyT3: ボルケザード / 6 starsフルッグ familyT3: ドラフルッグ / 6 stars

SS: I want to aim for the next one

The next lowest cost after the 6-star group. Select candidates for training according to the main lineup and roles that are lacking.

ガオデン familyT3: キングデン / 12 starsコロカメ familyT3: ガンタス / 12 starsヒマワリン familyT3: サーフクリン / 12 stars

Normal 18 star group

In principle, everything other than the above is in the normal cost range. Select by looking at the overall tier, purpose, current composition, and skills that will be added in the next evolution. We have not given Tatari an unreasonable S/A/B rating based on weak evidence.

How much priority should we give to the fourth evolution (T4)?

How to view T4 aura

In many 4-tier families, attribute auras are added at T4. Aura values are displayed from each Tatari T4 skill data.

  • Aura/Flame: Increases attack power of allies
    Example: Aura/Flame attack power increase initial value 2.5% / Aura/Flame attack power increase maximum value 12.5%
  • Aura/Thunder: Increases attack speed of allies
    Example: Aura/Lightning attack speed increase initial value 4% / Aura/Lightning attack speed increase maximum value 20%
  • Aura/Grass: Increases allies' HP
    Example: Aura/Grass HP increase initial value 4% / Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value 20%
  • Aura/Water: Continuously recovers allies
    Example: Aura/Water initial recovery rate 0.5% of target's HP / Aura/Water maximum recovery rate 2.5% of target's HP / Aura/Water recovery interval 4 seconds
  • Aura/Rock: Reduces damage taken by allies
    Example: Aura/Rock Damage Reduction UP Initial Value 3.5% / Aura/Rock Damage Reduction UP Maximum Value 17.5%
4th evolution aura is invalid in zombie rush

If you are aiming for a zombie rush, there is no need to prioritize T4 just because of its aura. However, it is possible that the basic status will change or be used in other content, so we do not treat it as meaningless to make it T4.
