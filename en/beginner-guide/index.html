1. What should I do first?

Start by collecting the freegift codesThen identify whether normal-stage trouble comes from timing out, a full defeat, or placement, and choose upgrade candidates using the verified beginner ratings and evolution priorities.

2. Advance through normal stages

If you can't win, deal with each symptom.

Time is up

Points to check: Insufficient firepower

  • Adopt a multi-lane attacker.
  • Move/add Tatari to the lane that cannot be defeated.
  • In some cases, there is an option to place something other than a tank fighter in the front row.

Annihilation

Points to check: Vanguard is not holding up/insufficient durability

  • Review the placement of healers.
  • Proceed with tank level and star reinforcement.

Placement

Points to check: Positioning is not engaged

  • Sometimes it can be improved by just lowering the whole area by one square.

4. Tatari that you want to grow first

This site has a beginner evaluation. This is not an official ranking, but a current provisional rating.

Snowcub Family Hold

The in-game skills are verified. Tier ratings remain pending because there is not enough battle evidence.

Frostnip Family SSS

Easy to aim for 3rd evolution at low cost. Easy to use from the beginning with slowdown, wide range attack, and penetration.

Blueflick Family SSS

Firepower for the early stages that allows you to quickly aim for the 3rd evolution. Causes damage mainly through combustion.

Frugling Family SSS

For the early stages where you can aim for the 3rd evolution quickly. As it evolves, the explosion frequency will increase and it will also gain deceleration.

Cheerling Family SS

Has recovery, offensive and defensive buffs, invincibility in a pinch, and fast recovery. Greatly improves stability mainly in zombie rush.

Buddi Family SS

Responsible for recovery and attack and defense buffs at the same time. Highly versatile as support for the entire formation.

5. First T3 candidate

Frostnip Family

Top priority candidate/6 stars

The number of stars required for 3rd evolution is small, making it easy to become a force from the early stages.

Blueflick Family

Top priority candidate/6 stars

The number of stars required for 3rd evolution is small, making it easy to increase firepower from the beginning.

Frugling Family

Top priority candidate/6 stars

The number of stars required for 3rd evolution is small, and it is easy to increase attack efficiency in the early stages with explosion frequency and deceleration.

Go to evolution priority

6. Tatari that can be used for a long time

Ashlarva Family

It has range firepower, recovery, and attack support that are easy to use in multiple contents.

Buddi Family

They are in charge of recovery and offensive and defensive support, and can easily become the basis of a long-term formation.

Punchimp Family

The increase in firepower after the third evolution is significant, making it a main candidate for long-term use.

Cheerling Family

Recovery, buffs, and survival assistance can be taken care of all at once, making it easy to have roles in multiple contents.

7. When you can't win

If the time is up, check firepower and lane, if wiped out, check the vanguard, recovery, and durability, and if placement, check positioning. If you want to match it to what you have, you can use the selection menu at the Guide Assistant.
