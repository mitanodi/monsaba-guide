Basics of strategy

Basic training order

  1. 3 vanguards to Lv7
  2. Main firepower Lv7
  3. Recovery/Support Lv7
  4. Strengthen the rest

Concept of resurrection

  • Use the same battle points for leveling up and resurrecting.
  • In high waves, it is difficult to recover if you start hitting repeatedly.
  • Basically, give priority to a "no-death" formation with thick vanguard, recovery, and CC.

Recommended formation: 3×3+1 (left and right division)

In Zombie Rush, two people share the six horizontal rows. The basic rule is that you are in charge of the three rows on one side and do not disrupt the roles of vanguard, center guard, and rear guard. However, rather than having a fixed arrangement, think about moving it according to bosses and special zombies.

When a boss appears, processing takes priority over formation.

In high waves, if you leave a boss behind, it overlaps with the next boss, making it easy to be unable to handle it all at once. When a boss appears, concentrate your firepower and defeat it quickly, even if you temporarily break the formation. The purpose is not to continue defending a fixed formation.

High Wave Positioning Technique: Blast Furnace Zombie

Blast furnace zombies have a long range, and if they remain in their normal position, they will not come forward and take time to process. Temporarily lower the Tatari of the lane that appears and guide the enemy forward. When they come forward, return to your original line and quickly deal with them with a concentrated attack.
