About privacy and access analysis

We will explain the access analysis used to improve the site.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights, and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand page usage and display speed and to improve the site.

These features are measured through the Vercel system that distributes this site. In addition to page display, navigation, internal links, search results, filters, Tatari comparisons, strategy consultation/question board usage, external links, and clicks on advertisements and affiliate links may be aggregated.

In GA4, information such as page views, traffic sources, usage environment, and operation events defined by this site is sent to Google through the Google tag. Google may use cookies and similar identification technologies.

Site search terms/bulletin board search terms, question/answer/reply text, arbitrary names, friend UIDs, Tatari names, open questions, posting IDs, and IP addresses will not be sent to this site's own Analytics event. Search uses only aggregate values that do not identify individuals, such as the number of characters and number of results, and excludes query strings from the page URL and referrer URL passed to GA4. Your selections will only be saved in your browser and will not be sent to our server.

Saving in the device

The selections you have for strategy consultation, the deletion of friend posts and question board posts, information for resolved operations, and A/B test assignments for future activation are saved in the localStorage of the browser you are using. A/B testing is currently disabled and will not save personal information such as your name or contact information when enabled.

your roster/evolving stages of "My Monsaba" and the saved teams of the Team Builder are also saved only in the browser's local storage and are not sent to the server of this site. Only if the user creates a shared URL, the recipient of that URL can view the team data. The Tatari name, Tatari ID, evolution stage, and team details you have will not be sent to this site's own Analytics event.

Friend Recruitment Board

The Friend Recruitment Board saves the posted UID, arbitrary user name, level, comment, and date and time of posting. Posts will be automatically removed from the list after 30 days.

To prevent spam, we do not store the posting source IP address itself, but use irreversible identification values to the extent necessary. Please do not post any personal information other than your in-game UID.

Clash of Critters Q&A Board

The question bulletin board saves and publishes the posted question title, text, category, arbitrary name, supplementary information such as the type and level used, answers, reply text, arbitrary name, and posting date and time. Please do not write personal information in questions/answers/reply.

In anti-spam measures, raw IP addresses are not stored, and an irreversible identification value using secret information on the server side may be used only for controlling posting intervals. Posters can delete their posts using the deletion information saved on their device, and questioners can mark them as resolved.

Reports for each post are saved for review by the administrator, and the post will not be automatically deleted just by reporting. The administrator will delete questions, answers, and replies that violate usage rules. Only anonymous information such as category, operation type, reply level, number of items, etc. is sent to Analytics on the question board, and title, body, answer, reply, name, search term, post ID, reply ID, and IP address are not sent.

About Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4. How Google handles informationGoogle Privacy Policy. Users can manage related settings inGoogle advertising settings.

Displaying official X posts

On the top page, public posts from Clash of Critters Official X (@monsaba_jp) are displayed by querying the X API from our site's server. X API authentication information is never sent to the viewer's browser.

To display posted images, connect to the image distribution domain of X. The handling of information after selecting a posting link will be in accordance with X's privacy policy. If we can't retrieve the post, we'll just show you the regular link to the official X.

A8.net affiliate program

Affiliate advertisements will be displayed as "PR" to distinguish them from regular strategy information.

This site uses the A8.net affiliate program operated by Fun Communications Co., Ltd. If an application or purchase is made via an advertising link, the operator may receive compensation.

A8.net and advertisers may use cookies and similar identification technologies for purposes such as displaying advertisements and measuring performance. The handling of personal information, etc. entered at advertising link destinations will be in accordance with the privacy policy established by the linked business entity.

This site itself does not directly acquire personal information such as names, addresses, and contact information of those who apply via A8.net.

Regarding preparations for introducing Google AdSense

This site may implement Google AdSense in the future. At this time, we do not have a Google AdSense ad distribution code installed, and we do not distribute advertisements using this service.

When introduced, third-party distributors such as Google may use cookies and similar identification technologies to distribute advertisements, measure effectiveness, and prevent unauthorized use. You may also see personalized ads based on your browsing history, etc.

The userGoogle advertising settingsYou can manage your personalized advertising settings. When actually implementing AdSense, check the applicable consent requirements and Google's latest policies and update this page again.
