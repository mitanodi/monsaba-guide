Is Zaplet strong?

You can increase the firepower of your allies by increasing the damage they take to the enemy. Easy to use in zombie rush as a range support role.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Zaplet → T2 Synthhog

  • Skill: パルスエコー → パルスリンク
  • Bat damage increase ratio: 15% (additional)
  • Bat Duration: 5 seconds (additional)

T2 Synthhog → T3 Sonarbat

  • Skill: パルスリンク → パルスバースト
  • Complete scar Additional damage ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Complete scar Maximum damage multiplier: 300% (additional)
  • Complete scar Duration: 3.5 seconds (additional)

T3 Sonarbat → T4 Umbraveil

  • Skill: パルスバースト → バットライト
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase maximum: 20% (additional)
