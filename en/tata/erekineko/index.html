What changes when it evolves?

T1 Kittazap → T2 Nekowatt

  • Cooldown reduction: 0.1 seconds per attack → 0.2 seconds per attack
  • Minimum cooldown: 3 seconds → 2 seconds
  • Damage range expansion: 1 square (additional)

T2 Nekowatt → T3 Voltazar

  • Kittazap Defense power increase ratio: 15% (additional)
