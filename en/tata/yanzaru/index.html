Is Punchimp strong?

A firepower frame that increases the number of clones through evolution. The increase in strength after the 3rd evolution is significant.

Should you evolve Punchimp?

  • T2→T3 priority candidate: The number of clones increases from 1 to 2. An important point where the number of clones increases and the firepower of the Punchimp Family increases greatly.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Punchimp → T2 Rockfu

  • Skill: サルトモ → サルダチ

T2 Rockfu → T3 Rockwu

  • Skill: サルダチ → サルシク
  • Number of clones: 1 → 2

T3 Rockwu → T4 Rockong

  • Skill: サルシク → 無限サル召喚
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP initial value: 3.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP maximum value: 17.5% (additional)
