Is Funglet strong?

It has attack speed enhancement, damage increase, and damage reduction. Highly contributes to the overall team.

Should you evolve Funglet?

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Funglet → T2 Trippet

  • Skill: ミニ胞子 → ミドル胞子
  • Duration: 4 seconds → 8 seconds

T2 Trippet → T3 Hyphoria

  • Skill: ミドル胞子 → メガ胞子
  • Spore Damage increase ratio: 10% (additional)
  • Spore Damage reduction ratio: 15% (additional)

T3 Hyphoria → T4 Ecstamira

  • Skill: メガ胞子 → ギガ胞子
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
