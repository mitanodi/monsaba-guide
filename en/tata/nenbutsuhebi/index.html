Is Taptail strong?

T3's high-speed sound waves penetrate infinitely and can firepower against multiple enemies in a row with a 3% stun. Provisional original evaluation of all modes SS before implementation of Season 1, taking into account the linear layout of the dojo, the dedicated support and range performance of the current ZR environment, and the ease of use for F2P and beginners.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Taptail → T2 Rattlecoil

  • Skill: 功徳ピストル → 功徳ライフル
  • Stun probability: 3% (additional)
  • Stun Duration: 1 second (additional)

T2 Rattlecoil → T3 Clangnaga

  • Skill: 功徳ライフル → 功徳キャノン
  • Number of high-speed sound wave penetrations: Infinite (additional)

T3 Clangnaga → T4 Dharmadder

  • Skill: 功徳キャノン → 功徳円満
  • Aura - Rock initial damage reduction: 3.5% (added)
  • Aura - Rock maximum damage reduction: 17.5% (added)
  • Aura effect for non-Rock Tatari: 50% (added)
