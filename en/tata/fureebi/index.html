What changes when it evolves?

T1 Shrimpyro → T2 Pyroprawn

  • Skill: 連発パンチ → 灼熱パンチ
  • Number of punches: 3 → 4
  • Burn Frequency: Every 8th punch (additional)
  • Combustion Damage multiplier: 20% (additional)
  • Burning Damage interval: 1 second (additional)
  • Combustion Duration: 5 seconds (additional)

T2 Pyroprawn → T3 Pyroclobster

  • Skill: 灼熱パンチ → 疾風灼熱パンチ
  • Burning target Additional damage multiplier: 15% (additional)
  • Number of punches: 4 → 5

T3 Pyroclobster → T4 Pyroviathan

  • Skill: 疾風灼熱パンチ → 極限灼熱パンチ
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase initial value: 2.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase maximum: 12.5% (additional)
