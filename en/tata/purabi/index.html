Is Cheerling strong?

Has recovery, offensive and defensive buffs, invincibility in a pinch, and fast recovery. Greatly improves stability mainly in zombie rush.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Cheerling → T2 Cheerlet

  • Skill: ラビットダンス → チアダンス
  • Pinch Activation condition: HP is 10% or less (additional)
  • Invincibility Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T2 Cheerlet → T3 Cheerspring

  • Skill: チアダンス → サンダーダンス
  • Fast recovery multiplier: 3% of target's HP (additional)
  • Fast recovery interval: 0.5 seconds (additional)
  • Fast recovery duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 Cheerspring → T4 Cheerstella

  • Skill: サンダーダンス → ライトニングダンス
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase maximum: 20% (additional)
