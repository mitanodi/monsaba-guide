What changes when it evolves?

T1 Dumbopus → T2 Dazopus

  • Skill: 身代わりタコ → 先陣タコ
  • Frequency of large water beads: every 6 attacks → every 4 attacks

T2 Dazopus → T3 Fishopus

  • Skill: 先陣タコ → 捨て身タコ

T3 Fishopus → T4 Lordopus

  • Skill: 捨て身タコ → フィッシュ＆キング
  • Aura - Water initial healing: 0.5% of target max HP (added)
  • Aura - Water maximum healing: 2.5% of target max HP (added)
  • Aura effect for non-Water Tatari: 50% (added)
