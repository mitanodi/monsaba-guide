Is コパンダ strong?

Continuous damage and slowdown caused by bamboo shoots. Excellent for holding back groups of enemies in zombie rush.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 コパンダ → T2 タケパンダ

  • Skill name: タケノコの術 → タケの術
  • Possible number of growth: 1 → 2
  • 3rd stage damage multiplier: 50% (additional)

T2 タケパンダ → T3 パンドウラク

  • Skill name: タケの術 → タケタケの術
  • Cooldown: 6 seconds → 4 seconds

T3 パンドウラク → T4 バンブオウ

  • Skill name: タケタケの術 → 竹海戦術
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
