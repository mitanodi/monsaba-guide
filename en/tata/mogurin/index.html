What changes when it evolves?

T1 Gopher → T2 Watchroo

  • Skill: モグタマ → サンドボール

T2 Watchroo → T3 Bastogard

  • Skill: サンドボール → サンドキャノン
  • Vision Obstruction Frequency: Every 14 attacks (additional)
  • Vision Obstruction Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 Bastogard → T4 Cratzar

  • Skill: サンドキャノン → サンドメテオ
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP initial value: 3.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP maximum value: 17.5% (additional)

Gopher Family skills

Evolution 1: Gopher

モグタマ

Fires a sand ball in a nearby area. If there is an enemy nearby, the attack will stop and you will gain "Culture/Damage Reduction".
