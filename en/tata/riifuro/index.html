Is Hootlet strong?

Suppress enemy movement with deceleration, attack speed reduction, and stun. Easy to use in formations that want to have a strong blocking role.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Hootlet → T2 Tikowl

  • Skill: パストゾーン → プリゼントゾーン
  • Stun Duration: 1 second (additional)

T2 Tikowl → T3 Chronerva

  • Skill: プリゼントゾーン → フューチャーゾーン

T3 Chronerva → T4 Hypnostrix

  • Skill: フューチャーゾーン → エターナルゾーン
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
