What changes when it evolves?

T1 Flameow → T2 Nekoflare

  • Skill: バチバチニャン弾 → アツアツニャン弾
  • Bounce frequency increase: every 5 attacks → every 4 attacks
  • Maximum bounce count: 2 → 3
  • Cat's bond Maximum number of expansions: 2 → 3

T2 Nekoflare → T3 Silversear

  • Skill: アツアツニャン弾 → メラメラニャン弾
  • Flameow Attack power increase ratio: 15% (additional)
