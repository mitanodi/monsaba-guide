Is ガオデン strong?

Assists in dealing with enemies by knocking back and increasing damage taken. He is a good candidate in situations where you want to push back.

Should you evolve ガオデン?

  • Priority candidate: 12 stars up to T3. After the 6-star group, you can aim for the third evolution at the lowest cost. The third evolution adds increased damage taken.
  • T2→T3 priority candidate: Increased damage taken is added. From the role of knockback, you can also support the firepower of the entire formation.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 ガオデン → T2 ガルルデン

  • Skill: ストレートウェーブ → アークウェーブ

T2 ガルルデン → T3 キングデン

  • Skill: アークウェーブ → サンダーウェーブ
  • Increased hit/damage increase ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Increased hit/damage duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 キングデン → T4 エンペラデン

  • Skill: サンダーウェーブ → エレクトロパルス
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase maximum: 20% (additional)
