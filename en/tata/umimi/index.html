What changes when it evolves?

T1 Droppit → T2 Bubbit

  • Skill: クラウドミスト → バニッシュミスト
  • Increased damage taken Increase ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Increased damage taken Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T2 Bubbit → T3 Tideon

  • Skill: バニッシュミスト → 裁きの霊海

T3 Tideon → T4 Haplysia

  • Skill: 裁きの霊海 → 裁きの霊域
  • Aura/Water Recovery magnification initial value: 0.5% of target's HP (additional)
  • Aura/Water Maximum recovery rate: 2.5% of target's HP (additional)
  • Aura/Water Recovery Interval: 4 seconds (additional)
