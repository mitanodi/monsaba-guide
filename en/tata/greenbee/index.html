What changes when it evolves?

T1 Goonbug → T2 Capobeetle

  • Skill: グラスダッシュ → フラッシュダッシュ
  • Damage increase ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Damage increase duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T2 Capobeetle → T3 Donscarab

  • Skill: フラッシュダッシュ → 閃光タックル

T3 Donscarab → T4 Beetleknight

  • Skill: 閃光タックル → 閃光突進
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
