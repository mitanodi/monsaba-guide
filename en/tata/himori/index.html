Is ヒモリ strong?

Firepower for the early stages that allows you to quickly aim for the 3rd evolution. Causes damage mainly through combustion.

Should you evolve ヒモリ?

  • Top priority candidate: 6 stars up to T3. The number of stars required for 3rd evolution is small, making it easy to increase firepower from the beginning.
  • T2→T3 Top priority candidate: Additional damage from enhanced combustion is added. Among the low-cost T3 sets, it is easy to increase combustion power.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 ヒモリ → T2 フレイモリ

  • Skill: フレイムボール → スコーチボール

T2 フレイモリ → T3 ボルケザード

  • Skill: スコーチボール → ブレイズボール
  • Enhanced burning damage multiplier: 20% (additional)
  • Damage interval: 1 second (additional)
  • Duration: 5 seconds (additional)

T3 ボルケザード → T4 インフェルドラ

  • Skill: ブレイズボール → サンブレイズボール
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase initial value: 2.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase maximum: 12.5% (additional)
