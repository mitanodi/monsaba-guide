Should you evolve Manteeny?

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Manteeny → T2 Stalkerix

  • Skill: 二連撃 → 三連撃
  • Number of damage: 2 → 3

T2 Stalkerix → T3 Serrabloom

  • Skill: 三連撃 → カマキリの舞
  • Number of damage: 3 → 6
  • Increased damage taken Increase ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Increased damage taken Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 Serrabloom → T4 Orchitoria

  • Skill: カマキリの舞 → サクラの舞
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
