Vanguard with continuous attacks and increased damage taken. Easy to use as a frontline role in the dojo.

Should you evolve コマキリ?

  • T2→T3 Depends on application: The number of attacks increases from 3 to 6, and an increase in damage taken is added. The number of consecutive attacks increases and debuffs to enemies are also added.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 コマキリ → T2 ハナキリ

  • Skill: 二連撃 → 三連撃
  • Number of damage: 2 → 3

T2 ハナキリ → T3 ヴァルキリ

  • Skill: 三連撃 → カマキリの舞
  • Number of damage: 3 → 6
  • Increased damage taken Increase ratio: 20% (additional)
  • Increased damage taken Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 ヴァルキリ → T4 サクランティス

  • Skill: カマキリの舞 → サクラの舞
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
