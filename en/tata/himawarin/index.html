Is Buddi strong?

Responsible for recovery and attack and defense buffs at the same time. Highly versatile as support for the entire formation.

Should you evolve Buddi?

  • Priority candidate: 12 stars up to T3. After the 6-star group, you can aim for the third evolution at the lowest cost. Increases recovery and offensive and defensive support.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Buddi → T2 Sunfleur

  • Skill: 温かい陽光 → 勇気の陽光
  • Sunshine/Offense/Defense Increase Increase ratio: 15% (additional)

T2 Sunfleur → T3 Solaflora

  • Skill: 勇気の陽光 → 必勝の陽光
  • Damage multiplier: 45% (additional)
  • Ratio against Tatari: 40% (additional)

T3 Solaflora → T4 Heliabloom

  • Skill: 必勝の陽光 → 凱旋の陽光
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
