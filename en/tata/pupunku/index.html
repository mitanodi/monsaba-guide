What changes when it evolves?

T1 Fumekit → T2 Sulfunk

  • Skill: ププガス → 引火ガス
  • Odor gas diffusion interval: 6 seconds (additional)

T2 Sulfunk → T3 Magmusk

  • Skill: 引火ガス → 爆発ガス
  • Odor gas diffusion interval: 6 seconds → 4 seconds

T3 Magmusk → T4 Blastniff

  • Skill: 爆発ガス → ガスの芸術
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase initial value: 2.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase maximum: 12.5% (additional)
