What changes when it evolves?

T1 Souphog → T2 Grillhog

  • Skill: 辛口定食 → ハンバーグ定食
  • Grill Shield Maximum shield value: 12%HP (additional)
  • Grill Shield Damage multiplier: 10% (additional)
  • Grill Shield Damage interval: 1 second (additional)
  • Grill Shield Duration: 6 seconds (additional)

T2 Grillhog → T3 Hothog

  • Skill: ハンバーグ定食 → 火鍋フルコース
  • Attack power increase ratio: 15% (additional)
  • Attack power increase duration: 5 seconds (additional)

T3 Hothog → T4 Searhog

  • Skill: 火鍋フルコース → カロリー爆弾
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase initial value: 2.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase maximum: 12.5% (additional)
