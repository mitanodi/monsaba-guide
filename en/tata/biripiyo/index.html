Is Sparkeet strong?

Increases attack power and reduces damage taken to allies at the same time. Support performance is very high.

Should you evolve Sparkeet?

  • T2→T3 Depends on application: Enhancement charging and attack power increase are added. Support performance for 3 allies will be further strengthened.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 Sparkeet → T2 Buzzbeak

  • Skill: ダブルリンク → デルタリンク
  • Number of friends to connect: 2 → 3

T2 Buzzbeak → T3 Toucanzam

  • Skill: デルタリンク → コアリンク
  • Enhancement charging/attack power increase increase ratio: 50% (additional)

T3 Toucanzam → T4 Technocan

  • Skill: コアリンク → エレクトロリンク
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase maximum: 20% (additional)
