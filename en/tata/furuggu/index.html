Is フルッグ strong?

For the early stages where you can aim for the 3rd evolution quickly. As it evolves, the explosion frequency will increase and it will also gain deceleration.

Should you evolve フルッグ?

  • Top priority candidate: 6 stars up to T3. The number of stars required to reach the third evolution is small, and the explosion frequency and deceleration make it easy to increase strategy efficiency in the early stages.
  • T2→T3 Top priority candidate: Explosion frequency is changed from every 4th attack to every 2nd attack, and deceleration is also added. Explosion frequency increases and even deceleration is added.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 フルッグ → T2 フルルッグ

  • Skill: ドラゴンフルーツ → ハウルフルーツ
  • Explosion frequency: every 6 attacks → every 4 attacks

T2 フルルッグ → T3 ドラフルッグ

  • Skill: ハウルフルーツ → ホーリーフルーツ
  • Explosion frequency: every 4 attacks → every 2 attacks
  • Deceleration reduction ratio: 30% (additional)
  • Deceleration Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T3 ドラフルッグ → T4 フルグロウン

  • Skill: ホーリーフルーツ → クロスボムフルーツ
  • Aura/Grass HP increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Grass HP increase maximum value: 20% (additional)
