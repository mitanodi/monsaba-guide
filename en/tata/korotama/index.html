What changes when it evolves?

T1 Humbug → T2 Humbleetle

  • Skill: 一球入魂 → 二球連投
  • Dung ball staying time: 9 seconds → 10 seconds
  • Dung ball upper limit: 1 → 2
  • Deceleration reduction ratio: 30% (additional)
  • Deceleration Duration: 3 seconds (additional)

T2 Humbleetle → T3 Zenscarab

  • Skill: 二球連投 → 三球猛攻
  • Dung ball staying time: 10 seconds → 12 seconds
  • Dung ball upper limit: 2 → 3

T3 Zenscarab → T4 Hierotyrus

  • Skill: 三球猛攻 → 全霊魂球
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP initial value: 3.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Rock Damage reduction UP maximum value: 17.5% (additional)
