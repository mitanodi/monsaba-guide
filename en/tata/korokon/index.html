What changes when it evolves?

T1 Sparkit → T2 Flametail

  • Skill: 火の玉 → 花火
  • Number of diffusions: 8 (additional)
  • Diffusion damage multiplier: 10% (additional)

T2 Flametail → T3 Flarevix

  • Skill: 花火 → 炎火
  • Number of blooms: 16 (additional)
  • Flowering damage multiplier: 10% (additional)

T3 Flarevix → T4 Phantifox

  • Skill: 炎火 → 花火
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase initial value: 2.5% (additional)
  • Aura/Flame Attack power increase maximum: 12.5% (additional)
