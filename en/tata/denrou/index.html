Is パチルフ strong?

Close-range firepower with paralysis and invincibility. He can be used as a player on the front line who can stop and hit.

What changes when it evolves?

T1 パチルフ → T2 デンロウ

  • Skill: 残影の連撃 → 麻痺の連撃
  • Paralysis Damage multiplier: 15% (additional)
  • Paralysis Effect interval: 1.5 seconds (additional)
  • Paralysis Duration: 5 seconds (additional)
  • Bind: Cannot move (addition)
  • Short binding interval: 0.2 seconds (additional)

T2 デンロウ → T3 ライロウ

  • Skill: 麻痺の連撃 → 疾風の連撃
  • Invincibility: Immune to damage and interference effects (added)

T3 ライロウ → T4 ジンライロウ

  • Skill: 疾風の連撃 → 迅雷連撃
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase initial value: 4% (additional)
  • Aura/Lightning Attack speed increase maximum: 20% (additional)
