Data update policy

Verifiable information and site evaluation will be treated separately.

Display label

  • Official confirmation Contents confirmed in in-game announcements or in-game screenshots
  • Independent evaluation This site's provisional judgment based on confirmed data and public strategy information
  • Confirming Contents that are pending conclusion or DB reflection due to lack of evidence

Tatari name/evolution/skills

We have organized the names, evolution stages, skill descriptions, and numbers that were confirmed in the in-game screenshots provided by users.

Game Mode Guides

Public strategy information and data recorded on this site will be organized separately. Reference information will be displayed on the strategy page where the source is saved.

Tier evaluation criteria

Evaluation by tier and mode is a provisional evaluation unique to this site based on public strategy information by confirmed skill, role, and purpose. This is not an official ranking. The overall rating will not be automatically changed just because of the Zombie Rush specific changes.

Evolution priority criteria

We will use the required number of stars, confirmed evolution differences, strategy objectives, and evaluation by usage as the deciding factors. If there is a shortage of materials, priority will not be determined.

Update/verification date management

The publication date, update date, data confirmation date, target environment, and items awaiting confirmation are recorded separately as page management data. Git commit date is not treated as in-game validation date.

Unknown data

Names, effects, numbers, and strategy conditions that cannot be confirmed will not be corrected by guessing.

Evaluation pending

Pending evaluation does not mean weak. At present, there is insufficient evidence to rank them.

Communication of incorrect information

If you find any errors, please attach the publication page and proof of confirmation.Operator X.

Official creator assets

Some images use game assets supplied to creators by the Clash of Critters operations team. Their use does not make this an official guide, officially endorsed site, or official partner.
