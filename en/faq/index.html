Clash of Critters Strategy FAQ

Read the conclusion first and proceed to the required page.

Open the Guide Assistant

Who would you evolve next?

It changes depending on what you have, current evolution stage, required number of stars, and strategy objective. Check the evolution priority, and if you don't have enough material to make a decision, please select the conditions at the Guide Assistant.

View evolution priority →

Which attributes should be cultivated?

It is not possible to determine one attribute that is common to all. Check the content you want to challenge and the roles you are missing, and narrow down your choices from the attributes page.

View page by attribute →

Which one would you raise if there were two?

Compare attributes, usage tiers, roles, and evolution differences under the same conditions. Tatari comparisons can be shared via URL, and the comparison results page itself is not registered for search.

Compare two Tatari →

How should I view Tier?

Tier is not an official ranking but a provisional evaluation on this site. Don't just decide based on the overall results; also check the ratings for normal, zombie rush, dojo, and beginner usage.

Check how to read Tier →

Will Zombie Rush specific changes affect the overall tier?

The overall tier will not be automatically changed just because of a special change. After Season 1 starts, we will check the actual battle and reconsider the evaluation for each mode.

See Zombie Rush notes →

Who would you recommend for the first T3?

According to your roster evolution data, the Frostnip Family, Blueflick Family, and Fulgugu family are the highest priority candidates for 6 stars up to T3. It depends on what you have and your purpose.

See T3 recommendations →

Who should evolve T4?

Select T4 after checking the required number of stars and major changes. Only high-impact evolutions with sufficient evidence are listed in T4 Recommendations.

See T4 recommendations →

Does attribute aura work in Zombie Rush?

According to the current confirmed information, the 4th evolution attribute aura is treated as invalid in Zombie Rush. Emphasis will be placed on dedicated Lv3, Lv5, and Lv7 skills and basic performance.

See Zombie Rush walkthrough →

Which is the Voltfawn Family or the Cheerling Family?

If you need penetration, paralysis, and CC, the Voltfawn Family is a candidate, and if you need recovery, buffs, and survival support, the Cheerling Family is a candidate. Choose by strategy mode and missing role.

See comparison article →

We have prepared a list of paralyzed roles automatically extracted from the role labels of existing Tier DBs. Please also check the skill text on the individual page.

What badge dojos do you recommend?

It changes depending on conditions such as attributes, frontline maintenance, and single firepower. Please check the candidates by attribute and the idea of placement on the dojo page.

Candidates will change depending on the conditions and required role for each boss. Filter only the Tatari that match your verified criteria.
