Clash of Critters Paralyzed person/Tatari list by role

Compare the three paralysis role families of Clash of Critters based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is a paralytic role?

This page summarizes the families that include "paralysis" as a role in this site's Tier DB. Paralysis activation conditions, duration, and resistance may differ for each Tatari, so role names alone cannot be treated as having the same performance.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

It can be used for initial narrowing down when you want to compare formation candidates, including their role in stopping enemy actions. Candidates who have different roles such as firepower and penetration at the same time will be judged based on the confirmed skills and usage-specific tier on the individual page.

Judgment to lower priority

In situations where we have not confirmed the effectiveness of paralysis or the enemy's resistance, we will not prioritize it based on paralysis alone. Candidates whose activation conditions or evolution stage are unconfirmed will be put on hold within the range of confirmed information.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners should list not only the presence or absence of paralysis, but also the normal evaluation, beginner evaluation, and evolutionary stage that can be reached with the item in hand. I see it as a candidate if you lack a role early on.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
