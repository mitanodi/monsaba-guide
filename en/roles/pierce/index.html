Clash of Critters List of tatas by penetration and role

Compare the three types of penetration roles of Clash of Critters based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is a penetration role?

This site's Tier DB summarizes the families that include "penetration" in their role. For details such as the number of penetrations and targets, priority will be given to the confirmed skills listed on the individual page.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

This is a formation that you want to consider dealing with multiple enemies, and this is an entry point for comparing candidates for penetrating roles. Check not only firepower, but also status abnormalities and support roles.

Judgment to lower priority

In situations where the number of enemies, placement, and penetration conditions are unknown, we do not conclude that it is optimal just because it is a penetrating role. The evaluation of a single opponent needs to be checked separately as there is no dedicated Boss column.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners should prioritize the beginner evaluation and normal evaluation, and check on the individual page whether they can fulfill the required role before evolution.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
