Clash of Critters Recovery person/Tatari list by role

Compare four healing families by verified roles, mode-specific Tier ratings and evolution data, including Normal, Zombie Rush, Dojo and beginner use.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is a recovery role?

Our site's Tier DB summarizes the lines that include "recovery" in their role. The recovery target, recovery amount, and activation conditions are based on the skill description that can be confirmed on the individual page.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

When looking for survival aids for a formation, you can also compare buffs other than recovery and role labels such as invincibility. Depending on the usage tier, we will separate the situations in which the same recovery role is good at.

Judgment to lower priority

Recovery alone may not be a solution to running out of time, so it will be considered separately from the solution to insufficient firepower. We do not estimate recovery amounts or target areas that cannot be confirmed.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners are judged not only on stability, but also on whether they can compensate for the current lack of survival assistance by lining up the beginner evaluation, normal evaluation, and training cost.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
