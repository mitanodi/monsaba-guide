Clash of Critters List of buffs and roles

Compare the 8 buff role families of Clash of Critters based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is a buff role?

This site's Tier DB summarizes the families whose roles include "buff" or "support". Priority will be given to confirmed skills on individual pages for reinforcement targets, numbers, and activation conditions.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

This is useful when looking for candidates to support the entire formation and comparing the roles they have together, such as recovery, offensive and defensive support, and the tiers by purpose.

Judgment to lower priority

Buffs whose targets and increase amounts are unconfirmed will not be estimated. Check for overlapping roles, as increasing support roles may result in a lack of direct firepower or vanguard.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners are selected based on not only the overall tier of a single unit, but also the beginner evaluation and the lack of roles they have, and whether they can support the developed main forces.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
