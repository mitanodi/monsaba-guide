Clash of Critters List of Tatari by role with deceleration

We will compare the six families of Clash of Critters reducers based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is a speed reducer?

This page summarizes the families whose role includes "deceleration" in the Tier DB of this site. The target, numerical value, and duration of speed reduction refer only to the confirmed individual skill information.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

It can be used to extract candidates when you want to compare auxiliary characters related to enemy progress and attack frequency. It may also serve as a comparison for other status abnormalities or range functions.

Judgment to lower priority

If the amount of deceleration or effective target is unconfirmed, the effect will not be estimated. The common deceleration role alone does not determine the superiority or inferiority of different modes.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
