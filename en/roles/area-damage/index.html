Clash of Critters Tatari list by range firepower/role

Compare Clash of Critters eight range firepower families based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is range firepower?

This site's Tier DB summarizes the families whose roles include "range," "extensive range," and "sustained firepower." The number of targets, range, and duration conditions are based on the confirmed skill description on the individual page.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

Can be used when comparing candidates with consideration for responding to multiple enemies. Check whether it also serves another role such as penetration or abnormal status, and whether there are differences in tiers for each purpose.

Judgment to lower priority

The superiority of ranged roles is not determined in situations where the number and placement of enemies are unknown. For single opponents, there is no boss-specific evaluation column, so this list alone cannot be used to rank them.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

For beginners, prioritize the normal evaluation and beginner evaluation, and check whether the current formation is insufficient in dealing with multiple enemies before making it a candidate for training.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
