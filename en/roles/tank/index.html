Clash of Critters List of tank owners/roles

Compare four tank families by verified roles, mode-specific Tier ratings and evolution data, including Normal, Zombie Rush, Dojo and beginner use.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is the role of a tank?

This site's Tier DB summarizes the families that include "tank" or "vanguard" in their roles. For durability values and how to receive attacks, the confirmed information on the individual page takes precedence.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

It can be used to narrow down the candidates based on the formation in which you want to place a vanguard or durable role, and to compare roles that can be used together such as shielding, recovery, and obstruction.

Judgment to lower priority

In situations where the placement or enemy attack specifications cannot be confirmed, damage reduction cannot be determined based solely on the role label. If the main cause is lack of firepower, consider other roles.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners should check the normal evaluation/beginner evaluation and the evolution stage, and compare them in order of whether they can support the firepower role they have already developed.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
