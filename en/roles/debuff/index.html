Clash of Critters List of debuffs and roles

We will compare Clash of Critters 7 debuff roles based on confirmed roles, usage tiers, and evolution information. Normal, Zombie Rush, dojo, and beginner's usage are also listed.

Confirmed DBSite Analysis

What is the debuff role?

Our site's Tier DB summarizes the families whose roles include "debuffs," "increased damage taken," and "decreased attack speed." Regarding specific numbers, targets, and duration, priority will be given to confirmed information on individual pages.

Scenes that are easy to include as candidates

This is the entry point when looking for support roles that work on the enemy side and comparing them, including dual roles such as status ailments and area firepower.

Judgment to lower priority

Effects cannot be determined in situations where valid targets or duplicate rules are unconfirmed. Don't decide boss suitability or final firepower just by role label.

Concept of prioritizing beginners and training

View for beginners

Beginners should check the normal evaluation/beginner evaluation and the evolution differences of individual pages, and consider candidates that are easy to combine with the main product.

  1. Look at the beginner tier and normal tier and narrow down the candidates that match the mode you are currently using.
  2. Compare the current evolution stage and confirmed skills on individual pages.
  3. Prioritize insufficient firepower, obstruction, and survival support over duplication of the same role.
  4. Evolution Priorityto check the required stars and evolution changes before spending materials.
