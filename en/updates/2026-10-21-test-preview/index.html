Test-server preview · tentative date

October 21 update: test-server preview

A topic-by-topic preview of a new grass Tatari, Zombie Rush Season 3, Halloween, collectibles and balance changes. The date is tentative; live release is unconfirmed.

Source checked: 2026-10-14

October 21 is tentative. Live implementation is unconfirmed.

This article summarizes external wiki test-server information. Release timing, content and event dates may change. Check in-game notices for the current rollout status.

  • What to check before the update

New grass Tatari: シャイプ

  • シャイプ is listed as a new grass Tatari, with Zombie Rush-only skills for its evolution line.
  • Evolution names, skill multipliers, acquisition requirements and our own tiers are not established by this source.

Zombie Rush Season 3

  • Rankings reset; difficulties 5 and 6 are added, with adjustments to difficulties 1–4.
  • Starting difficulty is described as one below your highest difficulty in Season 2.
  • The preview lists new enemies, revised stages and a different boss order.
  • The 火山を制する者 avatar-frame reward is scheduled to change after Season 3. Rank requirements and the end time are unconfirmed.

Halloween: a limited pinball buff

  • The preview window is October 27–November 27. Exact times and server differences remain unconfirmed.
  • The usual double buff is replaced by いたずらタイム, which allows a special card draw.

Balance changes and fixes

  • Some chips and dedicated skills are due for adjustments; this roundup does not specify the new individual values.
  • ミストリア / 安眠ミサイル and エーテリファル / 永眠ミサイル: adjacent allies’ dedicated-skill damage is planned to lose the slow and sleep effects.
  • The effect on normal attacks is unconfirmed; the wiki’s additional interpretation is not treated as a confirmed rule.
  • A ホルリン-family 縦横無尽 issue that moves allies outside the placement area is scheduled to be fixed.
  • Dedicated-skill descriptions are revised. Gift-box collection is planned to be capped at 20 times per person per week.
  • Chapters 24–28 are listed for enemy-placement changes and additional enemies.

What to check before the update

  1. Record your highest Season 2 difficulty so you can compare it after the transition.
  2. Keep the current descriptions of your chips and dedicated skills for comparison with the updated in-game text.
  3. Check the in-game schedule and entry requirements before joining an event.
