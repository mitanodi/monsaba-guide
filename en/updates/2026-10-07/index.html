October 7, 2026 Integrated Data Update

Rechecked chips, evolution trials, dedicated skills, events, facilities and gift codes, with sources and uncertainty labeled separately.

What changed

  • Compared all 49 chip images, names, ranks and effects. Corrected nine effect values and in-team summon wording. Official September 23 evidence resolves three conflicts; 21 English names use official notices.
  • Added external evolution evidence for all 66 families and 198 dedicated Lv3/Lv5/Lv7 skills. Normal skills and T4 auras remain separate.
  • Added officially implemented Thunderpaw and Cinderclaw. Their images remain pending rather than reusing another evolution’s image.
  • Reviewed normal descriptions for all 242 forms, added 502 effect-popup values and Tsubaruka T4 details; 20 name conflicts retain existing game evidence. Five English names were supplemented, with both cat T4 names also checked against the official English notice; missing Chinese names fall back to Japanese.
  • Added two codes and filled three existing reward records. Public listing, expiry reports and unperformed redemption are distinct.

Unreviewed names and conditions remain complete Japanese source text; translations and missing numbers are not invented.

Events and facilities

running-party

Four players share distance and earned rewards. The older roulette has eight outcomes totaling 100%. The guide’s 6.47 m estimate depends on unverified bonus-spin rules and is not treated as a confirmed Raft Race expectation. Higher multipliers increase variance; they do not establish better average efficiency.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07

carnival-fest

Normal and doubled-reward states have different distributions, each totaling 100%. Jackpot: 0.60% / 1.00%; next doubled reward: 7% / 12%; miss: 30% / 0%. The doubled state is not just twice the normal reward. Unidentified reward icons remain unnamed.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07

island-treasure

Four-player teams capture territory for union points. External recruitment and battle schedules, fourth-week drink carryover, fatigue-based costs and daily/final reward timing retain their source caveats. The official September 9 notice awards a Banquet Hall with the first MVP Giftbox; the same hall shares boxes, displays achievements and grants visiting trainers random pinballs. MVP Giftboxes expire after five days and convert to Gold Bricks; team-kill Blitz Gifts were removed. Differing Japanese box/building terms are not asserted to be separate items. October 7 soap rewards and final Gold Bricks increasing from 10 to 65 are announced changes awaiting maintenance completion.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07 · Update history

zombie-siege

Hunt Coin cost follows the field multiplier, and points are shared by the team. Evolution bonuses are blue 0%, purple 2.5%, gold 5%, red 7.5%, rainbow 10%; the guide states elemental matchup does not apply. The aggregate expected value is withheld because its calculation and rounding are unclear.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07

analyzer

The analyzer is confirmed in the September 23 official update. External details: 50 analysis capsules per attempt, one collectible, normal/rare/super-rare probabilities 60%/35%/5%; duplicate refunds 1/2/5 or rare 2/4/10. Analysis capsules are separate from ordinary capsules. Spa and rainbow-form additions remain October 7 preview information.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07

boss-rally

Rank uses highest level and damage; progress carries over when the same boss returns. After level 99, maximum damage determines competition. Fish takes extra damage for five seconds after teleporting. Rockstar summons 15 minions and reduces incoming damage while they live; Runner uses one triple strike and summons miners; Drunk boosts all zombies; President removes one Tatari. September 9 history adds 10% of the base ranking-cookie reward per boss with all difficulties cleared, capped at 50%, rather than per difficulty or compound growth.

External guide evidence · 2026-10-07 · Update history

fishing-tournament

Four-player teams compete on fish size. Ranking gold coins use size/weight differences; personal silver coins accumulate, and multipliers affect silver only. Each rod has a 0.3% bait chance. Bait gives the team +1 level for ten minutes, with up to four used together. September 9 history adds repeat rewards after 20 million silver coins; their interval and contents remain unpublished.

サトリッサム → コモリッサム → フタリッサム → ファミリッサム

Preview: T2 requires 6 stars and one copy; T3 requires 18 stars, three stat increases and shared progress 70/4000; T4 requires 36 stars, rank B, progress 120/7200 and 15 copies. Incoming damage falls by 20% and healing received by 40%. Feigning death lasts 8 seconds, dealing 120% every 1.25 seconds; it initially requires 9 hits, then 3 more per activation. The rock aura ranges from 3.5% to 17.5%, halved for other attributes. Evolution differences and unlisted numbers remain unverified.
