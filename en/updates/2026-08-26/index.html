Clash of Critters 8/26 update summary

Snowcub・Zombie Rush Season 1・New T4・Balance adjustment

August 26, 2026 Implemented/Details currently being confirmed

Organized based on official update announcements and official posts after release. The start of Season 1, difficulty level 1 to 4, 25 rounds, 750 enemies, ranking, chips, operation time 3 hours, etc. have been confirmed in the official announcement. We are waiting for confirmation of the evolution name and in-game values of Snowcub.

Important: Adjusting 35 Tatari is a Zombie Rush exclusive skill.

This is not a change to the basic skills used in normal stages. Normally, it is not mixed in the skill DB and is managed as Season 1 exclusive data. The overall tier, normal, dojo, and boss rally ratings will not be changed by this schedule adjustment alone.

New Tatari “Snowcub” and 4th evolution

New Tatari with water attribute “Snowcub”

Snowcub and its evolved form have exclusive skills used in Zombie Rush. This does not mean that Snowcub itself is exclusive to Zombie Rush.

The existence and water attribute have been confirmed in the official post. T2/T3 names, normal skills, special skill details, numbers, and in-game images have not been confirmed, so they have not been added to the Tatari DB yet.

Added 2 4th evolutions

  • Water attribute: Dumbopus → Lordopus
  • Rock attribute: Taptail → Dharmadder

The name has been confirmed in the official announcement. Skill details and numbers are usually not guessed until you check the in-game screenshot.

Zombie Rush Season 1

Season and difficulty level

  • Introducing a season system, Season 1 is approximately 4 weeks long
  • Balancing Tatari and Chip each season
  • Chip system permanently installed
  • Difficulty level up to 4. Clear it to unlock the next one
  • 1 game 25 rounds/750 enemies
  • If you defeat 750 monsters before the time runs out, you can challenge the next difficulty level.

Ranking and results

  • Season ranking determined based on highest difficulty level and number of kills
  • Season-limited icon frame can be obtained
  • You can check the match record up to a certain number of times recently.
  • Season results can be shared

Changes to event/strategy functions

魔法の農場リメイク

  • Change event cycle
  • Removed crop harvesting and feeding and reflected total weight directly to milestones.
  • Up to 5x critical when using fertilizer
  • Help function to invite your friend Tatari
  • Weight increase bonus based on invited Tatari star rank, evolution, feeding, and shiny level
  • Crops remain until the start of the next farm event

Surprise Roulette

  • Added 1 level of roulette
  • Changed from automatic to manual level up after winning a jackpot

Zombie rush optimization

  • Operation time changed from 2 hours to 3 hours (accumulation time is 1 hour)
  • Fixed a bug where the entrance may not be displayed when logging in for the first time.
  • Reduced number of kills required for reward
  • Pursuit possible even after the operation is completed
  • All participants can earn rewards after finishing
  • Optimize matching to prioritize trainers of similar level
  • Show recommended teammates above offline friends

Food processing plant/auto battle

  • Elaborate bento box: Cookie 15 → 9
  • Elaborate lunch box with attributes: Cookie 20 → 9
  • Optimized stage progress display for auto battle.
  • If disabled for a long time, tap to display instructions to enable it.

Stage strategy

  • Increased marble rewards after Chapter 22 to the same level as early chapters.
  • Compensation of differential rewards according to current progress

Bug fixes

An official notice has been posted to fix a bug that caused volcano zombies to revive under certain circumstances.
